## Routine schedule

- Monthly: review storage health, backups, Windows updates, and driver issues.
- Quarterly: inspect dust, filters, fans, temperatures, and cable condition.
- Before firmware changes: back up important data and record the current version and settings.
- After every material change: rerun the relevant portion of the validation plan.
